*Academic Tutor — Università di Modena e Reggio Emilia · October–December 2025*

Not a course I took: material I **authored and taught**. Nine lecture decks written for the
*Informatica* tutoring programme, supporting first-year students through programming fundamentals in
Python.

## 🎯 Skills demonstrated

Explaining is the hard test of understanding. Building this sequence meant working out where beginners
actually get stuck — scope, mutability, off-by-one errors in nested loops — and designing the
explanation around the misconception rather than the syntax. Alongside the decks, the role involved
live code review and one-to-one debugging with students.

Free to reuse for teaching; attribution appreciated.
